Belt & sheave calculator

Enter sheave ODs and center distance to get belt length, the right stock belt and drive speeds.

Drive inputs

Sets the driven sheave OD from the driver OD and speed ratio. Round to a stock sheave afterwards.

Results

    Enter sheave outside diameters (OD). Pitch diameter is OD minus 2a: 0.25 in for A, 0.35 in for B, 0.40 in for C, 0.05 in for 3V, 0.10 in for 5V and 0.20 in for 8V. Speed ratios use pitch diameters. Classical belt length is figured on pitch diameters and matched to pitch length (belt number + 1.3 in for A, + 1.8 in for B, + 2.9 in for C). Narrow belt length is figured on OD and matched to effective length (belt number ÷ 10). Length uses the exact open-belt formula. Stock sizes vary by manufacturer, so confirm in your supplier’s catalog and allow take-up for installation and tensioning.
